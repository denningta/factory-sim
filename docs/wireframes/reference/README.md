# Visual reference (product owner, 2026-11-24)

`control-room.png` is the look the UI is being redesigned towards (sprint 8 onward). Take from it:

- a dark control-room theme: near-black background, dark panels with thin borders, a teal/emerald accent used sparingly as glow
- the isometric 3D scene as the centrepiece: pale low-poly equipment on a dark floor with a faint grid, the active or bottleneck unit glowing in the accent colour, glowing connection lines
- floating callout cards with leader lines that show each unit's key figures
- a top strip of KPI tiles with icons, a right sidebar of ranked progress bars, and a bottom row of trend charts

Don't copy its content: the labels are placeholders from an unrelated energy dashboard. Our states, colours for meaning (working, starved, blocked, waiting for tech, walking) and SI units still apply, through the tokens (ADR-0016).
